Some common questions before you send your request.

How long does it take? Can I choose a date?

Basic and Plus requests are completed within 1 week. Preferred dates and live video calls are exclusive to Complete Premium. Premium dates must be confirmed based on Gurdwara Sahib availability.

What should I confirm before paying?

Ask for the coordinator’s name and contact details, the agreed date and scope, an itemised total, payment instructions and the receipt arrangements. Confirm cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms in writing before making a payment.

How are additions charged?

Additions are extra at the prices available at the shop. Items listed in your package are not charged twice. Extra quantities, item prices and the total are confirmed in writing before you agree.

What if my date or arrangements are unavailable?

Availability must be checked before confirmation. Ask which alternatives are possible and agree what happens if arrangements change, including any costs or refund terms, before paying.

Is this an official Gurdwara website?

This website presents MERI SUKHNA’s request coordination service. Gurdwara names and photographs identify locations; they do not establish endorsement or an official partnership. Confirm any claimed affiliation directly with the relevant Gurdwara Sahib.

Can I see the visit live?

A live call can be requested with the Complete option. It depends on permission, network coverage and scheduling. Ask what update will be provided if a call cannot go ahead.

Will I receive a video?

We can share a recording where permitted. Recording is never guaranteed in restricted areas; agree the available update format before confirming.
