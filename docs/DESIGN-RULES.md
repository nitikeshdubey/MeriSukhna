## Meri Sukhna design rules

Apply these owner preferences to every new or revised public, family and staff interface. Review changed screens against this list before release.

1. Avoid tags and pill-shaped badges.
2. Avoid excessive italics.
3. Do not use Instrument Serif.
4. Do not use negative letter spacing.
5. Keep interfaces simple and avoid excessive detail.
6. Use spacing instead of repeated dividers.
7. Use consistent solid palette colors; avoid decorative shades and opacity layers.
8. Avoid bordered content containers.
9. Keep diagrams simple.
10. Remove verbose or redundant text.
11. Avoid code-style breadcrumbs.
12. Do not number sections decoratively.
13. Omit eyebrow labels above headings.
14. Do not use dithering.
15. Do not use ASCII images.
16. Do not use squircles.
17. Avoid excessive monospace typography.
18. Do not use all-caps subtitles.
19. Use solid backgrounds instead of gradients.
20. Omit periods at the ends of titles.

Use sentence case, informative headings and clear controls. Preserve accessible focus indicators, form affordances, semantic navigation and machine-readable SEO metadata.
