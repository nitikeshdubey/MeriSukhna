Clear answers, before you decide

How can we help?

Your arrangements should feel clear from the start. Read the practical details, or ask us directly.

How long does it take? Can I choose a date?

Basic and Plus requests are completed within 1 week. Preferred dates and live video calls are exclusive to Complete Premium. Premium dates must be confirmed based on Gurudwara Sahib availability.

What should I confirm before paying?

Ask for the coordinator’s name and contact details, the agreed date and scope, an itemised total, payment instructions and the receipt arrangements. Confirm cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms in writing before making a payment.

What if my date or arrangements are unavailable?

Availability must be checked before confirmation. Ask which alternatives are possible and agree what happens if arrangements change, including any costs or refund terms, before paying.

Is this an official Gurudwara website?

This website presents MERI SUKHNA’s request coordination service. Gurudwara names and photographs identify locations; they do not establish endorsement or an official partnership. Confirm any claimed affiliation directly with the relevant Gurudwara Sahib.

Can I make a request from outside India?

This service is for families living outside India, Pakistan and Bangladesh. Availability is checked before the website and local prices are shown.

What if I already know the Gurudwara Sahib?

Just tell us the name when you send your request. We will check the arrangements and guide you.

Can you help with Akhand Paath Sahib or Sukhmani Sahib Paath?

Yes. Tell us what you want to arrange and we will explain the available options, process and price.

Do you provide Rumala Sahib?

Yes. We can purchase the Rumala Sahib and take it to the requested Gurudwara Sahib according to its maryada and instructions.

Can I add Deg / Karah Prashad?

Yes. Deg / Karah Prashad can be included in your request.

Can you arrange Ardaas with a Granthi Singh Ji?

We can request or coordinate this where available and subject to the Gurudwara Sahib's arrangements.

Can I request Langar or food distribution?

Yes. You can request Langar-related arrangements or distribution of fruits, samose, paranthe and other agreed items, subject to the Gurudwara Sahib's arrangements.

Can I see the visit live?

A live call can be requested with the Complete option. It depends on permission, network coverage and scheduling. Ask what update will be provided if a call cannot go ahead.

Will I receive a video?

We can share a recording where permitted. Recording is never guaranteed in restricted areas; agree the available update format before confirming.

Can I make the request for someone else?

Yes. You can make a request for your parents, grandparents, spouse, children or another family member.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you want to arrange and the Gurudwara Sahib involved. We will tell you the price before you confirm.

What can I expect regarding my Sukhna and my request?

Our focus is to carefully arrange your request and keep you updated throughout the process.

How are additions charged?

Additions are extra at the prices available at the shop. Items listed in your package are not charged twice. Extra quantities, item prices and the total are confirmed in writing before you agree.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?

Terms depend on the agreed arrangements and have not been published as a fixed policy. Ask the team for written payment, cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms before accepting a quotation or paying.

How does family access work?

The request owner can nominate a family member’s email, choose access to updates, the gallery or a Premium call, and set an expiry. The family member signs in with that email. Access can be revoked. Invitation delivery is manual.

How is my gallery shared?

Completion photos and videos are private. Signed-in customers and authorised family members receive temporary viewing links. Please keep downloads private and only share with permission.

Can I save my package?

Save a draft on this device to return later. Anonymous drafts exclude your notes. Signed-in customers can also save a private account draft when secure accounts are connected.
