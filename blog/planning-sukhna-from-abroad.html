Punjab door hai? Plan your Sukhna from abroad

Living abroad can make a simple family arrangement feel complicated. A clear written request helps everyone understand what you want to arrange, what is possible and what happens next.

By MERI SUKHNA · Published 20 October 2026

Start with your own request

Before choosing a package, write down what you would like to arrange. You may have a particular Gurdwara Sahib in mind, an offering you wish to discuss, or a request on behalf of a family member. If you are unsure, say so. Asking for guidance is a useful starting point; you do not need every detail ready.

Choose the place before promising a date

Share the Gurdwara Sahib’s full name and city. Similar names can refer to different places, so confirm the location rather than relying on a photograph. The arrangements allowed depend on that Gurdwara Sahib’s maryada and availability. A requested service should only be treated as agreed after those details have been checked.

Match the package to the support you need

MERI SUKHNA offers Basic, Plus and Complete Premium coordination. Basic and Plus have a completion timeframe of within one week. Preferred dates and live video calls are available only with Complete Premium, and must be confirmed. Choose based on the coordination and participation you need; a higher package does not imply a greater spiritual outcome.

Ask for one written summary

Before paying, ask for the agreed location, arrangements, quantities, timing, package cost, extra charges and update format in one message. Additions are charged separately at the prices available at the shop. Ask what payment record or receipt you will receive, and confirm cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms in writing.

Agree how your family will be updated

A recording or live call depends on local permissions and practical conditions. Ask what update is possible if recording is restricted or connectivity fails. Let one family member coordinate the request to reduce conflicting instructions. Punjabi jaan English — use the language in which you can explain your wishes most clearly.

This guide explains practical coordination. Confirm religious practice and local requirements with the relevant Gurdwara Sahib.
