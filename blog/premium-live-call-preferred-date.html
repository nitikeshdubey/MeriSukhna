Joining from abroad: preferred dates and live video calls

Being able to join a visit from abroad may matter to your family. A little preparation helps set realistic expectations about the date, the call and the update you will receive afterwards.

By MERI SUKHNA · Published 20 October 2026

Choose Complete Premium for these features

Preferred-date requests and live video calls are exclusive to MERI SUKHNA’s Complete Premium package. Basic and Plus follow the standard completion timeframe of within one week. A preferred date is a request until availability is confirmed; choosing it on the website does not reserve a slot.

Confirm the date and call time together

Once a date is available, agree the actual call time in Punjab and where your family lives. Put both in the same WhatsApp message, especially if family members are joining from different countries. If your date is flexible, give alternatives so the coordinator can check what is possible.

Permissions come first

A live video call should only take place where the Gurdwara Sahib permits it. Premium coordination does not override restrictions on recording, devices or access. Ask which parts of the visit may be shared and avoid assuming that every part of an arrangement can appear on camera.

Keep the family call simple

Choose one family contact, confirm who would like to join and agree how the call will be initiated. Keep your phone charged and available around the agreed time. Avoid sharing private joining details publicly. If you need a particular accessibility arrangement, explain it during the enquiry rather than waiting until the visit.

Agree an alternative update

Network coverage, device problems or a change in local arrangements can interrupt a call. Before confirming, ask what happens if a live connection cannot go ahead. Agree whether a permitted recording, a written update or another available format will be shared, and confirm any relevant cancellation or service terms.

Make the request without sharing sensitive information

The package page prepares a WhatsApp message containing your choices. Review that message before sending. You do not need to include passwords, banking details or personal documents to explain a request. The first step is simply to discuss your preferred place, arrangements and date.
