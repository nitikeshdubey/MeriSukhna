Rumala Sahib, Deg and Langar: planning your additions

Small details matter when someone is arranging a request on your behalf. Rather than selecting every option, start with the arrangements you actually want and confirm exactly what each addition means.

By MERI SUKHNA · Published 20 October 2026

Separate package arrangements from additions

Review the package details first. If an item is already part of your agreed package, selecting it again should not automatically request another quantity. Tell the coordinator whether you are confirming an inclusion or requesting something extra. The written quote should make that distinction clear.

Rumala Sahib: confirm the purchase details

If requesting Rumala Sahib, ask what can be accepted at your chosen Gurdwara Sahib before buying or requesting a particular item. Agree the purchase budget, the item details and any shop-price difference. Do not assume that an item shown online is available or suitable for every location.

Deg / Karah Prashad: agree the scope

For a Deg or Karah Prashad request, ask which arrangements are available and who will handle them. Confirm the agreed quantity or contribution and its cost. The local Gurdwara Sahib’s instructions take priority over a general package description.

Langar and food distribution need specific details

Langar seva and a separate food or fruit distribution request can involve different arrangements. Explain what you have in mind, then confirm the permitted items, quantities, location and timing. A broad instruction such as “food distribution” leaves too much open to interpretation. Ask for the final scope before confirming.

Paath is an optional addition to discuss

On our selection page, you can add Paath arrangements and choose the type you would like to discuss. That selection is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. Availability, scheduling and the final cost must be checked with the relevant Gurdwara Sahib.

Confirm extra charges before proceeding

Additions cost extra at the prices available at the shop. Ask for item prices, quantities and the total extra amount before authorising the purchase. If the available price changes, request an updated total. Keep a copy of the agreed message so both sides can refer to the same arrangement.
